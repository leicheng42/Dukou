# Dukou 0.1.18

Direct third-party destinations are now available in the Share menu.

## New

- Add **Show directly** to supported custom-app rows.
- Add direct **Send to Doubao** and **Send to Today** entries to WeChat's "Choose an App on Your Mac" menu.
- Use each target app's icon and open the exact destination without the extra custom-app picker.
- Keep generated Share helpers in Dukou's Application Support folder and register them only while their switches are enabled.

## Notes

- This community pre-release is manually installed and is not notarized by Apple.
- Direct entries are available only for apps with a signed helper template shipped in this build.
